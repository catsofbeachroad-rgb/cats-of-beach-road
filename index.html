About us

Who runs Cats of Beach Road, and why.

Our mission

Make sure every cat on Beach Road is fed, healthy and safe, and run it openly so the whole neighbourhood can trust where the money goes.

Our vision

A neighbourhood where community cats are cared for by the people who live alongside them, not feared or chased away.

Why it matters

These cats live here whether or not anyone looks after them. Steady feeding, sterilisation and vet care mean fewer kittens, healthier cats and a calmer estate for everyone.
